About

From physical assumptions to trustworthy simulation results.

I focus on the full computational chain: governing equations, discretization, implementation quality, convergence, and validation against known references. My goal is not only to run simulations, but to make the numerical logic transparent, verifiable, and useful for engineering decisions.

Alongside industrial tools, I build and document custom CFD solvers to deeply understand performance, stability, and model limitations. I value clean architecture, benchmarking discipline, and clear technical communication.
