About

From numerical-method development to industrial simulation.

I am completing an M.Sc. in Computer Simulation in Science at Bergische Universität Wuppertal. My work combines engineering physics, scientific programming, validation, automation, and clear technical documentation.

I build projects as reviewable engineering case studies: the problem is defined, assumptions are documented, results are analysed, limitations are stated, and the implementation is organised so others can reproduce the work.

CFD solver development

MATLAB Lid-Driven Cavity Study

Built and automated a 72-case SIMPLE-style CFD study covering three meshes, Reynolds numbers from 100 to 1000, two convection schemes, two pressure solvers, and loop/vectorized implementations.

Result: 44 cases passed the selected Ghia benchmark thresholds, while the remaining cases were retained to document numerical limitations.

Pedestrian dynamics

JuPedSim Evacuation Analysis

Developed six configurable evacuation scenarios using a real building geometry, six spawn regions, nine exits, parallel agent placement, Numba-accelerated exit selection, and SQLite trajectory output.

Result: Created a repeatable workflow for comparing exit assignments, pedestrian speeds, evacuation times, and simulation runtimes.
