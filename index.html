Lid-Driven Cavity Solver Comparison

Multi-language benchmark for the 2D incompressible lid-driven cavity problem across MATLAB/Octave, Python, C, C++, OpenMP, MPI, and a CUDA prototype.

Current dataset: 8 execution-complete CPU solver groups, 36 configured cases per group, and a current lowest median runtime of 236.76 s. Convergence reporting and the final benchmark protocol are still being refined.
