About

CFD-focused engineer with experience from numerical methods to industrial simulation.

I am completing an M.Sc. in Computer Simulation in Science at Bergische Universität Wuppertal. My work focuses on pressure–velocity coupling, solver verification, multiphase and thermal-fluid simulation, scientific programming, and structured post-processing.

I build projects as reproducible engineering case studies: the problem is defined, assumptions are documented, outputs are validated, limitations are stated, and the complete implementation is organised for others to review.

MATLAB Lid-Driven Cavity Study

Built and automated a 72-case SIMPLE-style CFD study covering three meshes, Reynolds numbers from 100 to 1000, two convection schemes, two pressure solvers, and loop/vectorized implementations.

Result: 44 cases passed the selected Ghia benchmark thresholds, while non-passing cases were retained to document numerical limitations.
