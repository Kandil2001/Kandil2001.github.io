About

From physical assumptions to trustworthy simulation results.

I focus on the full computational chain: governing equations, discretization, implementation quality, convergence, validation, and clear communication of limitations. My goal is not only to run simulations, but to make the numerical logic transparent, verifiable, and useful for engineering decisions.

Physics first

I start from the engineering problem, the assumptions, and the boundary conditions before moving into software, code, or visualization.

Validation mindset

I look for reference solutions, mesh effects, residual behavior, runtime trends, and experimental comparison where possible.

Reproducible workflow

I prefer structured outputs, documented runs, version control, plots, and case studies that can be checked and improved later.
