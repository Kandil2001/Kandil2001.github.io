Profile

I work where CFD, numerical methods and scientific programming meet. My development of CFD codes started during my Bachelor’s graduation project, where I built a numerical model for heat-transfer analysis. During my Master’s, this developed further through solver projects, benchmarking and high-performance computing.

I have practical engineering CFD experience with ANSYS Fluent, STAR-CCM+, OpenFOAM and simulation validation work. My main interest is the software side of simulation: writing solvers, checking results against benchmarks, automating workflows and comparing performance.

I have worked on industrial CFD cases and research topics involving airflow, heat transfer, multiphase flow and controlled environments. Alongside my work, I develop CFD codes in C, C++, Python and MATLAB, including serial and parallel versions with MPI, OpenMP and CUDA basics.
