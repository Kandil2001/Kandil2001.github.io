Profile

I work where CFD, numerical methods and scientific programming meet. My development of CFD codes started during my Bachelor's graduation project, where I built a numerical model for heat-transfer and airflow in controlled environments. Since then, I've combined practical engineering with hands-on solver development.

I have practical engineering CFD experience with ANSYS Fluent, STAR-CCM+, OpenFOAM and simulation validation work. My main interest is the software side of simulation: writing solvers, checking convergence, understanding discretization, and validating results against theory or experiment.

I have worked on industrial CFD cases and research topics involving airflow, heat transfer, multiphase flow and controlled environments. Alongside my work, I develop CFD codes in C, C++, Python and MATLAB, and I'm comfortable with parallel programming (MPI, OpenMP, CUDA basics).
