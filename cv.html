Profile

I work at the intersection of CFD, numerical methods, scientific computing, and engineering validation. My background combines hands-on industrial simulation work with custom CFD code development, allowing me to understand both the practical use of commercial tools and the numerical logic behind them.

My main focus is building trustworthy simulation workflows: starting from physical assumptions and boundary conditions, moving through discretization and solver setup, checking convergence and mesh effects, and validating results against reference data or experiments whenever possible.

I am especially interested in CFD software development, simulation automation, thermal-fluid systems, multiphase flow, controlled environments, and HPC-based performance studies. I use C, C++, Python, MATLAB, MPI, OpenMP, Linux tools, and CFD software such as STAR-CCM+, ANSYS Fluent, OpenFOAM, SimScale, and FDS.
